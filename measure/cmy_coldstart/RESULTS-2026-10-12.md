# The c2 cmy stimulus and its cold start (2026-10-12)

The questions (src/holo/receiver/docs/CMY-RECEIVER-PLAN.md A9; docs/CMY-DESIGN.md §11 G12, R17, R19): what the
suite's c2 page (`_h3_sims_cmy`, `c2-cmy-r0775x0700@10x10` at 2400 dpi, e043c sheet 3's bars, M and Y +1.0 dot in x,
a 3-px dot gain) reads like beside the paper it imitates; whether the whole page closes under the plants Z, A and B;
and what the production receiver does with a 1x2, 2x2 or 2x3-tile scrap of it, nothing certified.

**Answers, measured (single synthetic page, no paper -- NO promotion):**
- The dot gain is tuned as far as one binary dilation goes: the supervised context-free W row reads 0 % at 0-2 px,
  **5.88 % at 3 px (shipped)**, 47.7 % at 4 px, against 13.9-14.6 % (e043c B1/B14) and 14.9 % (e044 A). The sim's
  label bits read hi 2.15 % / lo 1.69 % hard, paper hi 3.2-3.4 % / lo 3.9-4.2 %: the sim is EASIER than paper. Its
  W errors go mostly to M (3.05 of its 5.88 % of W cells; C 1.62, Y 1.21) where paper's go least to M (e043c B1/B14,
  e044 A and S17 U12: M 3.2-3.6 % of W cells against C 4.5-5.1 and Y 5.3-9.0; S14 B3 least to C, 1.3 against M 2.5,
  Y 4.3); its Y->M is 3.93 % of Y cells, paper's 0.5-1.1 % on all five rows.
- The whole page closes VERIFIED byte-exact under Z (0, 0), A (0.5, -0.5) and B (1.0, 1.0) dots, 0 wrong
  certificates, by `tiles.scan` and `tiles.cond`, the field never solved before the close.
- Every scrap of every shape is placed at its true counters, every certificate is the generator's (0 wrong; 889
  LDPC attempts over the 21 scraps, CRC exposure <= 2.1e-7), and no scrap closes (12 metadata equations at most).
  Under Z, A and B every tile of every scrap certifies on both streams. **Under C (2.5, -2.5) no scrap certifies
  anything, on any shape -- R17's expected recovery is refused: a FINDING.** M has no field data there (its 2.5 dots
  past the anchor tier's 2.24-dot reach), Y reads -1.6 to -1.9 on one or two tiles (a false lock: a wrong value 0.6-0.9
  dot from the plant's -2.5 that cleared its null floor and was published), `tiles.swim` attempts and fails.
- What recovers the displacement: under Z the scraps certify by scan before the field solves; under A mostly so; under
  B nothing certifies before the first field solve on any scrap, then scan, cond, local and swim (on the 1x2-B-drift
  scrap swim minted all four certificates).
- The field reads M within 0.04-0.08 dot of A's +0.5 and Y within 0.01-0.09 of B's +1.0; M at B reads 0.06-0.18
  short, shorter the smaller the scrap. **Y's reading leans about 35 % toward M's offset wherever the two differ:**
  at A, Y reads -0.13 to -0.16 in the same-blur control and -0.14 with no interpolation at all (plant -0.5), -0.33 to
  -0.40 under the drift; scratch decodes (below) put Y +0.14 for a +0.5 plant beside M -0.5, and -0.65 for -1.0
  beside M 0. **The cause, measured (`lean.py`, below): M's INK in the B channel.** The raw per-template measurement,
  before any solve, already leans 33-37 % of (dM - dY) and equals the decodes' published field within 0.02 dot, so
  the solve is not the cause; without the dot gain it leans the same 34-38 %, so the gain is not the cause; with M's
  ink cleared from the render Y reads its plant within 0.01 dot; with the cross-talk weights dropped the lean
  reverses, 46-47 % AWAY from M. `field_tiers.CROSS_TALK_STATES` weights M's ink out of Y's template only at M's own
  cells. Inferred, not separated: Y's samples are free of M's ink only where the sample grid lines up with M's cells,
  and the NCC normalisation rewards that alignment. On paper the pull would be small where M and Y sit together
  (e044 A: M-C +1.26, Y-C +1.18; reader arithmetic).
- The drift is not resolved: across a 2-tile scrap the field's per-tile x range is 0.08-0.11 dot against the
  plant's +-0.25 tile-row means -- the field (smoothing length 5.56 mm, one c2 tile 5.42 mm) reads the scrap's mean.
