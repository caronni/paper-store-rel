# What the colour row's held-out log loss actually does

2026-09-20, branch `sched-instrumentation`. Four bounded traced decodes
on paper plus one colour sim. MEASUREMENT ONLY — nothing here is a
ruling, and the colour row's adoption rule is unchanged on the default
path.

## 0. The question

`ColorEvidenceRow.heldout_logloss` is consumed, not ignored:
`tiles.color_candidates` (plugins/tiles.py:262) puts it FIRST in the
ownership order key `(loss, extent, region, plane)` and
`lib/owners.freeze_page_owners` gives each contested payload cell to the
first writer in that order. Its own comment carries the measured record:
*"Ranking by a purely geometric key cost 27 e029 + 27 xerox
certificates."*

But the cell's FOLD (`store_folds._fold_color_row`) is bare
change-detection last-write-wins: any difference in loss, values or
cells adopts, and the newest row stands whether it is better or worse.
API.md's cell-class law calls this addendum 14's fourth decision cell
and says no disposition has been ruled.

Germano asked three things: has this been tried before, is the loss
jittering or monotone, and would "only an improvement adopts/wakes"
lose anything.

## 1. The record — what the docs already say

**It has been tried, and the ORDER is measured-causal.**
`docs/archive/holo2/kparity/DIVERGENCE-LEDGER.md` row 1 is the
controlled experiment. v1 ordered payload ownership by
`(heldout_logloss, patch_id)` with the CTX-CORRECTED loss; holo2 ordered
by a fixed geometric pose key. Measured twice, independently: **26–29/30
e029 + 27/48 xerox tiles gained, 0 regressions in 1,910 + 1,882**.
Controls make it causal — reversed order 4/30 and REGRESSES 64; a hash
coin 20/30; holo2's geometric key 0/30, *worse than random on the gap
population*. The marginal (pre-ctx) loss gives 22/30 and a per-tile
local loss 24/30: only the post-ctx per-WINDOW loss reproduces v1.
So the loss is load-bearing at the ORDERING step. Nothing in that record
speaks to the FOLD.

**The sibling cell is known to be non-monotone.** API.md's addendum-14
text, for the `region ctx` cell, records the one open ruling: *"ctx
quality is measurably NON-MONOTONE over growing evidence (0.0201–0.0216
oscillation), so a later fit made on a larger evidence base can score
worse and be refused."* The K side already pays for coupling adoption
to quality: `_fold_ctx_outcome` deliberately DECOUPLES them — *"a re-fit
that scores WORSE still hands the standing offsets to input windows
carrying no correction yet. Coupling them starved late windows and cost
274 certificates on e029 (bc730a0, 2026-08-30)."* That is the closest
thing in the tree to a measured answer on "only improvement adopts", and
it is a warning.

**The analyzer's own discipline says a loss like this is not a clean
held-out number.** `analyzer/calibrate.py:96` and `:905`: every arm that
MINIMISES an objective over the search fold has a
`heldout_logloss_search` that is *"a minimum, not a held-out number"*,
and `select_arm` refuses to report it as nested CV; the 2026-09-06 review
widened the refusal to every arm that chose its own knobs. DESIGN §4.8:
*"never the minimum"*.

**Three properties of the receiver's number, read off the code:**

1. It IS a held-out split — `color_region_head.window_loss` scores the
   parity-1 sites and `_search_mask` fits on parity-0 (:172, :381).
2. But the HEAD was selected over candidates (own / registration-
   corrected / pooled) on the lane's held-out labels, so the per-window
   number is scored under a winner chosen on the same fold. It is the
   analyzer's own "winner minimised the select fold" case, at window
   granularity.
3. **The label set GROWS.** `build_window_rows` labels a cell from
   format truth (furniture) PLUS this run's certificates
   (`certified_projection`). Every re-fit is therefore scored on a
   LARGER and DIFFERENT label population than the row it is compared
   against. That is exactly the comparability question addendum 14's law
   says a decision cell's schema must answer once — and for this cell it
   is unanswered. A rising loss can mean "the head got worse" or "harder
   labels are now in the held-out set", and the number alone does not
   say which.
4. A window with no labelled held-out site inherits the LANE's loss as a
   fallback (`window_loss`'s `fallback` argument), so those windows are
   all tied and their ownership falls through to the extent and region
   tiebreakers.

## 2. The instrument

`src/holo/receiver/sched_trace_folds.py` (new, sibling of
`sched_trace.py` at the 1000-line seam) writes one line per fold of
`ColorEvidenceRow` and of the K analogue `WindowEvidence` to
`<run_dir>/sched-trace/folds.jsonl`, only under `HOLO3_SCHED_TRACE=1`:
wall clock, kind, row key, whether the fold adopted and why not,
standing and new loss and their delta and direction, how far the values
moved MEASURED ON THE CELLS BOTH ROWS CARRY, what cells came and went,
the tile keys the row covers, the fact keys the fold moved and the
number of worker bookmarks those keys were about to wake.

Two call sites in `store_folds`, each behind one
`if sched_trace.TRACE.enabled:`; the subscriber count is filled in by
`_Engine._ingest` right after the fold, because `_index` lives on the
engine. OFF path untouched — the byte-identity test
(`test_tracing_changes_no_byte_of_the_decode`) still holds.

Reducer: `measure/sched_trace/logloss_report.py <run_dir>` (one run) or
`… <dir> <dir> … --compare` (the A/B).

## 4. What the loss does

### The colour row: two folds, one transition, no jitter

| sheet | colour folds | rows | folds per row | improved | worsened | equal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H | 1,424 | 712 | **exactly 2, every row** | 688 (96.6%) | 24 (3.4%) | 0 |
| I | 1,946 | 973 | **exactly 2, every row** | 853 (87.7%) | 120 (12.3%) | 0 |

Every row is written twice: once by assembly (the standing head applied
to a debt window, with context bins counted over that batch alone) and
once by its lane's single re-fit. **Zero folds were refused as
identical** on either sheet — last-write-wins never had a tie to break.

Because there is exactly ONE transition per row, JITTER IS NOT
OBSERVABLE HERE AND CANNOT OCCUR: sign changes 0 on both sheets, by
construction. The colour row is not being jittered around; it is being
written twice, and the second write is the designed replacement
(`color_region.assembly_job`: *"the next genuine re-fit sees the whole
lane and replaces every row written here"*).

Magnitudes (nats of held-out log loss):

| sheet | median abs Δ down | p90 down | median abs Δ up | p90 up | median net Δ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H | 0.0512 | 0.0974 | 0.00731 | 0.0179 | −0.0499 |
| I | 0.1064 | 0.3040 | 0.0688 | 0.3527 | −0.0897 |

On H the worsenings are an order of magnitude smaller than the
improvements and 17 of the 24 are below 0.01 nats — the size of the
0.0201→0.0216 oscillation API.md already records for the sibling ctx
cell. On I they are the same order as the improvements.

### The K row DOES jitter — and only a K sheet can show it

`calibrate` and `ctx` never fire on a cmy8 sheet, so **`WindowEvidence`
has ZERO fold records on H and I.** The K cell was measured instead on
one traced K-sim decode (`sim/decode-k.24057`, `c3-k-r0550@7x7`,
workers=1, 48 certificates, close VERIFIED):

* 58 folds over 10 rows — **up to 10 folds on one row**, not two;
* 48 re-emissions: 29 improved, 19 worsened;
* **38 sign changes** — the direction flips on nearly every step.

One row's whole life, verbatim:

```
0.0367 → 0.0313 → 0.0367 → 0.0307 → 0.0367 → 0.0302 → 0.0367 → 0.0297 → 0.0294
```

That is a PING-PONG, not noise: an uncorrected value (0.0367, unchanged
to four places every time) alternating with a context-corrected one that
genuinely improves each cycle. Every fold after the first changed only
the READ leg (`reason: read` — `ctx_adopted` / `heldout_logloss` /
`printed_llr`), so the fit underneath never moved. Half of this cell's
re-emissions put back a strictly worse row, and they bought 360 of the
935 subscriber wakes the cell's folds caused (38.5%). SIM EVIDENCE, one
small page — the paper K sheets are not in this campaign.

### The values, conditional on the loss

The values move on essentially every re-emission, in BOTH directions:

| sheet | loss | folds | median frac of cells changed | median max abs Δ (LLR) | median mean abs Δ | cell set changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H | improved | 688 | 1.0000 | 10.68 | 1.234 | 0 (0.0%) |
| H | worsened | 24 | 1.0000 | 9.87 | 1.037 | 0 (0.0%) |
| I | improved | 853 | 1.0000 | 13.83 | 1.893 | 370 (43.4%) |
| I | worsened | 120 | 1.0000 | 13.55 | 1.857 | 82 (68.3%) |

So a worsened row is NOT a row that barely moved: it carries a decoder
input that differs on every cell by the same order as an improved one.
There is no case anywhere in 3,370 colour folds of a row whose values
were identical and whose loss alone moved (`values still` 0 everywhere).

**Only some worsenings are even refusable.** A row whose CELL SET
changed must be adopted under any rule (the cells are its contribution
to the tile cover). On H all 24 worsenings have an unchanged cell set,
so all 24 are refusable; on I only **38 of the 120** are — the other 82
changed their cell set.

**Ownership is live almost everywhere.** Tiles covered by two or more
rows of the SAME lane — the only tiles whose cut a loss change can move:
H **3,496 of 4,551 (76.8%)**, up to 6 writers on one tile; I **4,690 of
5,395 (86.9%)**, up to 9. Nearly every loss-moving fold touches at least
one contested tile (702 of 712 on H, 973 of 973 on I). The loss is not
decorative: it is deciding cuts.

## 4b. The wake consequence

Colour-row key emissions woke 128,546 worker bookmarks on H and 122,044
on I — about 1.9% and 1.8% of each run's total wakes (6.63 M / 6.79 M),
so the colour cell is not where the engine's wake traffic lives.

Of those, the emissions from rows whose loss did NOT improve:

| sheet | adoptions | subscribers woken | not-better adoptions | their subscribers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H | 1,424 | 117,493 | 24 (1.7%) | 3,429 (2.9%) |
| I | 1,946 | 105,960 | 120 (6.2%) | 12,599 (11.9%) |

Those wakes are the upper bound on what an improvement-only rule could
save, and they are small: ~3.4 k of 6.6 M wakes on H. The `H-improve`
run confirms the mechanism end to end — its not-better adoptions and
their subscriber wakes are both **0**. On the K sim the same figure is
360 of 935 (38.5%), an order of magnitude larger share.

## 5. The A/B

`HOLO3_COLOR_ROW_ADOPT` (a MEASUREMENT hatch, read once at store
construction; `last` is the shipped fold, byte-identical):

| run | refused | reasons | certified at stop | certified at matched wall (485.1 s) | job cpu | tiles.scan | tiles.retry | tiles.local |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-last | 0 | — | **2,147** | 2,147 | 4290.5 s | 11,341 / 1031.1 s | 22,193 / 2211.1 s | 332 / 250.9 s |
| H-improve | 24 | `refused_improve` | **2,145** | 2,145 | 4308.2 s | 11,310 / 1034.8 s | 22,084 / 2208.9 s | 332 / 271.4 s |
| H-nonworse | 9 | `refused_nonworse` | **2,140** | 2,140 | 4289.4 s | 11,363 / 1076.8 s | 23,113 / 2302.6 s | 240 / 201.5 s |

**THE A/B IS INCONCLUSIVE AT THIS BOUND, and the runs say so
themselves.** `improve` and `nonworse` differ only on an exact tie, and
there were NO ties in any run (0 `equal` folds) — so the two arms
implement the same rule on this material and should agree. They differ
by 5 certificates (2,145 vs 2,140). That 5 IS the noise, measured, and
the spread across all three arms is 7 (0.33% of 2,147). `last` sits
inside it. Per plane the arms even move in opposite directions (C
595/591/589 falling while Y 213/222/225 rises), which is what
scheduling noise looks like, not a rule effect. Job CPU agrees to 0.4%
and the decoder fire counts to within 4%.

The refusal counts themselves are run-dependent for the same reason:
`H-last` and `H-improve` both saw 24 worsenings with an identical median
net Δ of −0.0499, while `H-nonworse` saw 9 — its re-fits ran against a
slightly different certificate set, which is exactly the "different
evidence bases" problem of §1.3.

What the A/B DOES establish: the hatch works, the refusals happen where
the trace says they should, and refusing them costs nothing measurable
in 480 s. What it does NOT establish: whether refusing them costs
something at close. The 480 s stop is at 2,147 of 5,883 tile-streams —
36% of the way in — and the rows an improvement-only rule refuses are
assembly rows read in a superseded feature space, whose damage (if any)
would show up in the long residue, not here.

## 7. What this does and does not settle

**Settled by this campaign:**

1. `heldout_logloss` IS consumed, and its ORDERING role is measured-
   causal from an earlier campaign (26–29/30 + 27/48 tiles gained,
   0 regressions, controls included). Nothing here disturbs that.
2. The colour row is **not jittering**. On two paper sheets every row is
   written exactly twice, 0 sign changes, and the second write improves
   the loss 96.6% (H) / 87.7% (I) of the time. A lane fits its head once
   — confirmed over a 36-minute run as well as these bounded ones.
3. The K `WindowEvidence` row **is** jittering, measurably: up to 10
   folds on one row, 38 sign changes in 48 re-emissions, a clean
   ping-pong between an uncorrected and a context-corrected value, with
   38.5% of the cell's wake traffic spent putting the worse one back.
   SIM ONLY, and it is the cell API.md's ctx note already warned about.
4. "Only an improvement wakes" would, for the colour row, refuse 24 of
   712 re-emissions on H (all refusable, 17 of them below 0.01 nats) and
   38 of 973 on I (the other 82 worsenings changed their cell set and no
   rule may refuse them). The saving is ~3.4 k of 6.6 M wakes.

**NOT settled, and the reasons are structural:**

* **The losses being compared are not comparable.** Labels are format
  truth PLUS this run's certificates, so every re-fit is scored on a
  larger, different held-out population than the row it displaces. A
  rising loss may mean a worse head or a harder label set; addendum 14's
  law says a decision cell's schema must answer this once, and for this
  cell it is unanswered. **Any improvement-only rule adopted before that
  question is answered is refusing rows on a number that does not
  compare.**
* The per-window loss is scored under a head SELECTED on the same
  held-out fold — the analyzer's own "winner minimised the select fold"
  case, which `select_arm` refuses to report as nested CV.
* The A/B at 480 s is inside its own noise (spread 7, and 5 of that
  between two rules that are identical on this material).
* The precedent cuts AGAINST coupling adoption to quality: the K ctx
  fold deliberately decouples them because coupling *"starved late
  windows and cost 274 certificates on e029"*.

**Recommendation (not a ruling).** Do not flip the colour row to
improvement-only on this evidence: the prize is ~2% of a cell that is
2% of the wake traffic, the numbers being compared are not on a common
footing, and a refusal keeps an assembly row read through a superseded
head. The K `WindowEvidence` cell is the one with a real, measured
last-write-wins pathology, and the cheap fix there is not a quality gate
either — it is to stop re-publishing the uncorrected row over the
corrected one. If the colour cell is to be converted, the ordinal should
be evidence-base-major (certificates at fit time, then loss), the same
shape `placement_ordinal` already uses, not quality-only.
