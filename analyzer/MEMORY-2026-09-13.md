# The analyzer's memory — where a long pass holds it, and what was done (2026-09-13)

**Trigger.** A ladder pass over the ten-capture sheet-J base on the 128 GB
machine 4 was killed by memory pressure after ~2.5 h. The only artefact was a
tee'd stdout that said almost nothing: the analyzer wrote nothing to disk
until a pass returned, so the kill lost every row and every mint. The
closest readable run is the 2026-09-10 pass on the same base
(`<volume>/J-all-20260910.73898-analyzer-all-20260910-153236.log`):
2,740 targets, 22 arms, 5 rules, 8 rungs, 14 workers, killed at 350 of
2,740 keys, +51 certificates, 2 h 10 m, 22.4 s per key.

**Method.** Six readers over the code on the `page-field-analyzer` branch
at `cd08a832`, each with a lens (rasters and crops; the patch family's
caches; the run's own per-target state; the memos; the snapshot and
`RunInputs`; the run record), allowed tiny synthetic measurements
(`tracemalloc`, `vmmap`, `nbytes`) and no ladder, no decode, no snapshot
read above 7 MB. Every number below is marked MEASURED (a unit size or a
real file) or ESTIMATED (arithmetic over measured units for the stated
run: N = 30 lanes = ten 2400 captures × three levels, T ≈ 2,700 targets on
two faces, `--workers 8`, `everything+patch+pure`). The only whole-run
calibration points are sheet H's (`everything`, 1,001 targets, 7
captures: 44.6 GB max RSS, 29.6 GB anonymous footprint — MEASURED) and the
sheet-I first cascade (341 targets: 10.4 / 8.1 GB).

Accounted: 21–46 GB anonymous plus 17–23 GB file-backed for the stated
run; the sheet-H calibration says the observed peak runs ≈ 2.4× the
accounted live allocation (arenas, BP and solve temporaries). At 350 keys
the ledger held only ~2 GB, so a kill there points at the two 4800
captures at 14 workers (patch banks ≈ 20 GB) or at another process; a
kill at 350 CERTIFICATES (~2,300 keys deep) is the ledger alone.

**What is not the problem.** Rasters: already memory-mapped, and the
right decision (loading them would cost 22.6 GB of dirty memory).
`--workers`: not the memory axis at 2400 dpi (≈ 1 GB for eight), only the
multiplier for the 4800 patch banks and the width of the pooled `field2`
fit (bounded by `field2.read_slots`).

**Mapping the snapshot from file.** Not possible as written: `snapshot.write`
uses `savez_compressed` (DEFLATE), and numpy 2.5.2 ignores `mmap_mode`
for any npz member, stored or deflated (MEASURED). It would need an
uncompressed `.npy` blob beside the spine at 9.3× the disk. Two smaller
receiver-side changes make it unnecessary: a zero-copy `_unpack`
(MEASURED bit-identical on all 926 arrays of the J fixture, read peak
3.24× → 1.19×) and `local_cells_yx` as int16 — together J 5.9 → 0.7 GB,
H 11.6 → 1.3 GB.

**A CPU finding beside the memory.** Per (key, lane) the battery cuts
19–21 crops where exactly two distinct ones exist (the 13 box arms share
one geometry, the patch/pure arms another; MEASURED byte-identical for
identical arguments): 654 ms against 71 ms on a Y tile at level 2, ≈ 3.2
CPU-hours on the stated run.

## 2. Ruled 2026-09-13 (Germano: "1, 2, the int16, and the CPU finding")

1. Stream the ledger and checkpoint — one change, two defects: the header
   before generation 0, each row appended as its generation retires it,
   readers last-write-wins per key and tolerant of a truncated last line,
   the generation batch freed as rows are built, `default_evidence`
   dropped; `mints.jsonl` appended per certificate; `checkpoint.json`
   every 30 minutes (generation, pending/retired/certified, the exposure
   accounting, timing, memory). Recovery is `export-facts` over the
   partial ledger into a new base, then a pass over the remaining
   residue; a `--resume` is a follow-up if that door proves clumsy.
2. A progress line every 50 keys and at every generation boundary: keys,
   mints, seconds per key, elapsed, peak RSS (`ru_maxrss`; `psutil` is not
   installed).
3. `Placement.local_cells_yx` int16 at its two producers, consumers
   unchanged (all coerce), archives still read as int64 — the receiver's
   own change, on this branch by his go; a golden that moves is a format
   fork and stops the change.
4. One crop per (lane, footprint, geometry), memoised for the life of
   one lane of one key (a per-key memo would hold twenty crops on a
   ten-lane key). LANDED: 13 cuts → 2 per (key, lane) on sheet I, the
   whole `blind_key` result identical but for timing; `refit.bind`'s
   closures still cut their own.

## 3. Deferred, with their prices

- A lean row: the five per-label arrays (`calibrate.py:~782-791`) are
  80–92 % of a row and only the live fit reads them; a `--lean-ledger`
  needs the offline-reader check first.
- `all_rules` off on a yield pass: 410 rung records per row, measured
  zero unique yield on J (`ledger.md` leave-one-out 156/156/156).
- `_MAX_CERT_SETS` 8 → 2 and an eviction on `arms_lane_mix`; the
  per-generation token also recomputes every projection and mix (CPU).
- The 4800 patch path: cap `FeatureBank._moments` and count `_selection`
  in the byte cap; hand `fit_population` float32.
- The receiver's zero-copy `_unpack`; a packed-bit mask instead of a
  coordinate list (61–119× on the field) — a snapshot format change.
- `arms_local_context` has no eviction and is per (lane, target,
  generation): a latent cliff under `--ctx-mode local5`.
- Record corrections owed: DESIGN §10 "Memos are per-thread" is false
  (no `threading.local` exists); CLAUDE.md's venv paragraph — `env/` on
  this checkout IS the free-threaded build.
