# W8 — the third-layer C wave, 2026-09-06: what was built, what it cost, what it did

One page. The working spec, the run matrix and the full trial record are
`APPROACHES-2026-09-06.md`; the measurement that started the wave is
`MEASUREMENTS-2026-09-06-C-THIRD-LAYER.md`; the design consequences are
`DESIGN.md` §14 (v0.4); the dated build log is `PLAN.md` §6. Every number

## 1. The day in one paragraph

Sheet H's C residue was measured to be one thing: a C cell whose siblings M
and Y are both inked reads two to four times worse than any other C cell,
sheet-wide, worst in the dead band, and the third-layer C has no dot core —
a flat red deficit at about 60 % of a normal dot's strength, spilling into
the neighbours — so a box aperture reads it badly and no spectral,
registration or threshold arm touches it. The answer was to learn the
aperture: a linear discriminant over the raw pose-resampled patch, per
lane, plane and sibling state, trained on the certified neighbours. With
that arm alone the 153 open C tile-streams of the morning's base went from
0 certificates (the default arm) to 96 in 93 seconds; the full patch
battery took that to 127; the fixed-point pass over the whole 200-tile
residue under `everything+patch` certified all 200 and closed byte-exact.
The analyzer ends the day with ten new modules, the run choosing its own
knobs, a pure path beside the integrated one, and its operator's doors;
main carries all of it (merge `a69ac42`).

## 4. Value against cost

- **The patch discriminant is the whole difference**: 0 → 96, for 1,276
  CPU seconds against the 1,436 the default arm spends over the same
  ladder finding nothing — 8.3 CPU s per tile, 0.8 s of wall per tile at
  the 10.4-way parallelism a pass alone on the machine reaches. One crop
  and one 144–576-dimensional solve per lane and state; 22–42 GB resident.
- **The per-state split pays only on the three-ink cells**, where it is
  the entire gap; the state-agnostic filter matches it on every ordinary
  state. It needs both siblings certified, which is why it fires in the
  dead band and not on the open live/marginal tiles whose Y is open.
- **RGB is the biggest single knob** (S1r → S1c, 0.171 → 0.127, R +0.10) at
  three times the features, and blind it is worth **+19 certificates**
  (R3-red 109 ⊂ R3-rgb 128).
  **"The self-discovery chose it without labels" was TESTED on 2026-09-07
  and is a claim about the RULE, not about the objective.**  The
  original sentence (rgb on 69 % of the three-ink lane-rows) was measured
  at the wide halo; at the halo the profiles carry since `cddcb87` the
  same walk picks **red on 85 %** of them and lands at 115, thirteen
  under the pinned rgb cell — and the halo × channel matrix says why: not
  filter quality but the walk's FRANCHISE (21 % of three-ink populations
  never voted and the ledger recorded the pin as a choice) and its BAR
  (2 SE moves with the label count; the walk measured rgb better on 60 %
  of the three-ink populations at halo 16 and refused it on 1,033 of
  them).  With the franchise restored and the burden of proof moved to
  the leaner side of a NESTED axis, the walk picks rgb on 95 % and lands
  at **127** — one short of the pinned cell before the ridge repair and
  ONE AHEAD of it after (RIDGE-auto 127, RIDGE-rgb 126, crossing) —
  *found by the run at half the pinned cell's CPU, and
  because the rule changed, not because the objective got better*: the
  objective is still scored on a population 4.3–4.8× easier than the
  cargo it prices.  Full record: `HALO-CHANNEL-2026-09-07.md`,
  APPROACHES §6.12.
- **16 samples per cell over 8** is the second knob (R +0.09) at four times
  the features.
- **L3f** adds 6 on its own — I6's 102 against I2's 96, the two passes that
  differ only in it, at 1.7 × the CPU (3,396 against 2,016). Its value
  **in composition is not measured**: X1 and X3 both mint 127 (X1 at
  75 % of X3's CPU, 27,744 against 36,838) but they ran on different
  patch filters, so that equality is a coincidence of counts and not an
  isolation — see §3.2's code-window paragraph. **Self-training** lifts
  R by 0.02 offline and COSTS five certificates blind, on the one clean
  pair in the set (X1 127 → X2 122, 1.21 × the CPU). **Affine
  registration** adds one certificate over X3 for 1.7 × the CPU (62,152),
  and this is the wave's cleanest A/B — X3 and X4 are consecutive runs on
  one code state.
- **`platt-state`, L7c, rings, the aperture variants and high label floors**
  earn nothing on this sheet; rings and the floors are harmful. **The blind
  mixture** minted no certificate either, but it is not the same negative:
  offline it takes the three-ink cells 0.238 → 0.214 (7 of 7 lanes past
  2 SE, APPROACHES §6.2) and leaves the live band alone. They stay in the
  toolbox as measured negatives.
- **The pure path** is real and strictly weaker: 50 certificates, every one
  inside X3, at 282 CPU s per tile before B8 — 34 × the patch arm alone
  and 1.6 × the plain battery per tile, for far fewer certificates. Its
  two variants land at 39 and 38 for still more CPU, but on different
  `pure.py`s, so 50/39/38 is not a variant ladder (§3.2). The level is
  the finding and it holds on every code state the path has had: 38–50
