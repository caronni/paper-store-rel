E021 DEFICIENCY FINDINGS
========================

Review date: 2026-07-13

Scope
-----

This review compared e019, e020, and e021; inspected their design records,
implementations, qualification scripts, reports, and generated PNGs; and read
the related Claude and Cursor transcripts.

The central finding is that e021 optimizes for a persuasive "9/9" result more
than for the stated deliverable: exact, independent areas containing
probably-valid payload. The coarse tensor survey -> pitch estimate ->
pitch-scale refinement architecture remains promising, but the current
implementation and evidence do not justify promotion as a completed stage-0
receiver.


1. THE QUALIFICATION IS WEAKER THAN ITS CLAIMS
----------------------------------------------

The document says "9/9 rows measured, all design claims hold," but several
rows test only existence or bbox overlap rather than the stated property.

* Q4 ribbons passes when any returned bbox overlaps a ribbon. It does not
  check pitch, c, angle, tier, mask accuracy, one-to-one ownership, or false
  regions.

  experiments/021-payload-regions/qualify_e021.py:221

* Q6 reports successful separation at gaps of 2.0, 1.0, 0.6, and 0.4 mm, but
  the actual pass condition checks only rows with gap >= 1.0 mm. The 0.6 and
  0.4 mm claims cannot make the battery fail.

  experiments/021-payload-regions/qualify_e021.py:261

* Q9 is called a head-to-head comparison, but its pass condition requires
  only that e021 overlap the healthy and faint boxes. It does not require
  correct boundaries, one-to-one regions, acceptable false positives, or an
  advantage over e019/e020.

  experiments/021-payload-regions/qualify_e021.py:379

* The direct e017 cross-format row records "found": True without invoking
  detect_payload_regions. It tests a private pitch function on a hand-picked
  crop, not platen region detection.

  experiments/021-payload-regions/qualify_e021.py:170

* The weak-line unit test is vacuous if no region overlaps the noise blob: its
  loop executes zero times and all([]) is true. It does not prove the claimed
  weak-line contract.

  tests/test_e021.py:135

* Most tests check that at least one bbox overlaps truth. They generally do
  not reject extra regions, splits, merges, poor masks, or incorrect ownership.

Consequently, "9/9" is not a reliable summary of segmentation correctness.


2. THE REAL OUTPUT DOES NOT MATCH "INDEPENDENT PROBABLE PAYLOAD REGIONS"
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

The documented real runs return these total region counts:

  scan                         physical pieces    returned regions
  confetti-1                         17                  29
  confetti-2                         10                  26
  confetti-3                          7                  16
  confetti-1-2ndtake                 17                  35
  confetti-2-2ndtake                 10                  31
  confetti-3-2ndtake                  8                  17

The excess is relabeled "marginal" and described as downstream's problem.
However, confident and marginal entries are returned together as
PayloadRegion objects. The API therefore does not return probable payload
regions; it returns a mixture of payload, scanner-border artifacts, paper
corners, dust, shreds, and other texture candidates.

  experiments/021-payload-regions.md:245
  src/paperholo/platen.py:273

The assertion that each false region costs only about one second and will be
rejected by downstream gates is not validated in e021. No pilot-lock or decode
qualification is run over all emitted real candidates.


3. THE ADVERTISED TWO-TIER DETECTOR IS NOT THE IMPLEMENTED ONE
----------------------------------------------------------------

The module description says confident texture is above max(noise floor, Otsu)
and marginal texture is above the lower floor. In the implementation:

* thr_conf is calculated;
* admission uses only thr_marg;
* tier is assigned later from pitch availability and contrast >= 30;
* the candidate's relation to thr_conf is not used to assign its tier.

  src/paperholo/platen.py:303
  src/paperholo/platen.py:326

Thus the code does not implement the documented two-tier texture semantics.
Otsu mostly does not participate in confidence classification.


4. E020'S CLEAN MODEL ACCUMULATED FAILURE-SPECIFIC RULES
---------------------------------------------------------

E021 claims to avoid arbitrary print-event-dependent constants, but currently
contains at least the following decisions:

* 340 um survey scale;
* four work pixels per assumed cell;
* 0.22-cell derivative sigma;
* 0.30-cell tensor sigma;
* 6-sigma and 12-sigma score floors;
* 5-of-9 density support;
* 9 mm2 component rejection and hole filling;
* 4 mm scanner-border demotion;
* spectral contrast threshold 30;
* absolute crop standard-deviation threshold 1.5;
* dominance threshold 0.7;
* refinement threshold 0.25 * field median;
* 256--1024 px pitch-window half-width;
* 1.5 mm refinement halo;
* 6 percent c-snap tolerance;
* default print_dpi=600 for c_dots.

Some may prove valid, but physical or dimensionless units do not make a
constant non-arbitrary. Many were introduced after observing failures on the
same scanner and scan set. Several do not appear in the design's constants
table.

The result is a substantial heuristic system rather than a clean consequence
of the structure-tensor model.


5. GEOMETRY AND SCOPE CONTRADICTIONS
-------------------------------------

Pitch window
~~~~~~~~~~~~

The code says the pitch-measurement window "stays INSIDE the piece" by using
the interior depth, but clamps the half-width to at least 256 native pixels.

  src/paperholo/platen.py:318

At 4800 dpi, the advertised 0.5 mm ribbon is about 94 pixels wide. A 512-pixel
full window cannot remain inside that ribbon and will mostly contain backing
or other material. Q4 checks only bbox overlap and therefore does not expose
this contradiction.

Dominance path
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

When the dominance score exceeds 0.7, e021 abandons independent-region
segmentation and emits one largest footprint component.

  src/paperholo/platen.py:291

The design admits that separate pieces next to a dominating sheet are absorbed
into that one region.

  experiments/021-payload-regions.md:350

That is a documented violation of the primary "independent areas" contract,
not merely a minor limitation.

Hidden configuration
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The API advertises dpi as the only required configuration, but c_dots assumes
print_dpi=600 unless overridden. Physical pitch can be configuration-free;
the c_dots interpretation cannot be unless print pitch is part of the format
contract or independently inferred.


6. "EXACT BOUNDARIES" WERE TRADED FOR TOPOLOGICAL CLEANUP
-----------------------------------------------------------

The refined structure-tensor masks contained many enclosed flat regions.
E021 now fills all enclosed holes below 9 mm2 and declares their pixels to be
payload.

  src/paperholo/platen.py:220

Enclosure is useful evidence that a hole lies inside a broader field, but it
is not evidence that modulation exists in that hole. The operation may make
the PNG and crop more convenient while contradicting the stronger claim that
the mask denotes exact signal-bearing area. It also reverses the earlier e019
principle that locally absent modulation should remain an honest erasure.

The real PNGs still show paper-margin excursions, border components, and
occasional regions whose bboxes reflect scrap geometry rather than only the
printed field. There is no exact annotation of the real masks against which
"true boundary" can be asserted.


7. THERE IS NO INDEPENDENT FINAL EVALUATION
-------------------------------------------

E020 explicitly separated calibration seeds 100--107 from held-out seeds
200--207. E021 repeatedly changed after inspecting the same qualification
fixtures and the same six real e018 scans:

* full-page failure produced the dominance branch;
* border artifacts produced border demotion and revised tier semantics;
* a flat crop produced the absolute std guard;
* mask speckles produced bounded hole fill;
* radial-pitch junk produced fallback deletion.

The same scans were then rerun and treated as validation. There is no held-out
real scanner, paper stock, backing, contrast level, or independently annotated
mask set.

Cursor's later removal of _radial_pitch is particularly informative: the
original 9/9-certified implementation had assigned confident tiers to eight
junk regions. The user had to question why two pitch methods existed before
the active hazard was measured and removed.


8. TEST AND ARTIFACT GAPS
--------------------------

* Tests do not consistently assert absence of extra regions.
* Most synthetic truth is bbox truth rather than exact payload-mask truth.
* There is no one-to-one assignment metric for split and merged regions.
* There is no real mask precision/recall or IoU annotation set.
* There is no end-to-end pilot-lock validation of confident and marginal
  candidates.
* The documented deliverable
  experiments/021-payload-regions/gen_fixture.py does not exist.
* The qualification report contains pass booleans and selected headline
  numbers, not the raw distributions needed to audit thresholds and errors.


10. WHAT REMAINS SOUND
-----------------------

The following ideas remain technically promising:

* a rotation-invariant structure-tensor survey;
* deliberate acquisition-footing decimation;
* coarse survey followed by local pitch estimation;
* pitch-matched boundary refinement;
* honest refusal when pitch evidence is weak;
* emitting physical pitch and an approximate angle as evidence rather than
  requiring c as detector configuration.

These are a good research direction. The deficiency is the gap between that
core and the present claims, API semantics, heuristic accumulation, and
qualification strength.


11. MINIMUM BAR BEFORE PRODUCTION PROMOTION
--------------------------------------------

1. Freeze the region semantics: distinguish survey candidates from
   probably-valid payload rather than returning both under one type/list.
2. Annotate real payload masks across multiple scans, stocks, backings, and
   contrast conditions.
3. Measure mask precision, recall, and IoU; one-to-one candidate precision and
   recall; split rate; merge rate; and false regions per platen for each tier.
4. Use held-out physical scans after all thresholds and branches are fixed.
5. Run downstream pilot/lattice lock over every emitted real candidate to
   validate the claimed false-candidate cost and confidence semantics.
6. Correct the pitch-window geometry for thin and small regions.
7. Remove, ablate, or independently justify every special branch and constant.
8. Align the design document, module contract, and actual tier implementation.
9. Keep e021 labeled as an experiment until these conditions are met.


Bottom line
-----------

E021 is not worthless and its central architecture may be the right one. It is
currently an overfit research prototype presented as a completed receiver
stage. Its scorecard does not measure the full stated objective, its real
output contains substantial admitted junk, and its documentation frequently
rationalizes those discrepancies rather than treating them as unresolved
engineering work.
