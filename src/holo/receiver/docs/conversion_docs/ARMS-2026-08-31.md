One entry per mechanism landed on main this day. Each carries: what it

## The field line (fields row + lib/plane_field + field_tiers + swim)

**Units fix.** The fitter measured in cells while every consumer, the
clamp, the publish floor and the diag band read dots. Adjudicated with
an injected-displacement trace at three cell_dots values, fixed with
ONE division inside the fit loop (fields owns no shifting function any
more, so the sides cannot drift again). Effective clamp had been 6
dots at c3 / 4 at c2; effective floor 0.75 / 0.50 dots. Post-fix the
displaced-sim Y field reads 1.024 dots against an injected median of
exactly 1.000. The pre-fix real-sheet consequence is recorded below
(machine 2, sheet I: 54 fits, zero publications).

**Three evidence tiers, one fit.** Photometric cells (rank-1 along the
local gradient, tile-centred), anchor sites (rank-2 isotropic
pseudo-observations of the delta itself, NOT centred — they measure
position, not level), blind dust-anchored per-window offsets. The
attribution law (ruled: no double counting): every site contributes
through exactly one tier — certified payload -> photometric, furniture
at even site parity -> anchor, odd parity -> blind; the partition is a
pure function of the cell. Tiers add as normal equations, so
leave-one-tier-out accounting is an exact subtraction, reported per
fit. The anchor tier's justification is the PAPER measurement (sheet I
vs recorded ground truth, 481 tiles: gain 0.94-1.00, per-column
Pearson +0.982, zero saturation on Y, where the photometric estimator
reads gain 0.53); on every sim the photometric tier dominates 122-163x
— the sims prove wired/exact/harmless, not value. The blind tier is
admitted at its natural weight (a held-out log-loss improvement, mean
0.005/obs vs the anchor's 0.999) and is currently near-inert; the
scale between the two kinds is a measurement nobody has made, and no
factor was invented. ANCHOR_GAIN = 1.0 rests on a frozen-template
response of exactly 1.0000, zero nonlinearity, all planes.

**Median publication + clamp order.** Each plane publishes
p − median(all three): plane-common bias cancels exactly AND a
single-plane signal leaks nothing into its siblings (the mean-of-
others form measured (0,0,D) -> (−D/2,−D/2,D), 0.45-0.56 dots of
spurious sibling publish on a decode). Order: subtract-then-clamp —
at measured scales clamp-first annihilates a 0.97-dot common part
against a binding ceiling.

**The swim (tiles.swim, 240×n — the dearest row).** Per-plane local
affines (6 params/plane/window), fitted by maximising a concentrated
Gaussian likelihood over PROVEN labels only (format furniture +
neighbours' certified cargo re-encoded through the format; the target
tile's own cargo excluded — the E8 self-reference, caught twice: once
in E8's record, once in this row's own first draft where a frozen
level table made the starting pose self-fulfilling). Field-hinted:
the search bound is measured from the standing field's value, which
buys reach past the field's clamp (4.043 dots recovered at a 4-dot
injection where the blind search clips at 3.0). Existence proof
re-bracketed TWICE as field repairs erased it (2 dots -> 3.5 -> 4.0);
at the final bracket the field-only arm misses one Y tile that
survived every cheaper rescue row and the swim recovers it
byte-identical to control. Licence table (field-as-applied vs swim,
per injection): the honest field is good inside its clamp and DEGRADES
past it (26% at 4 dots, 13% at 5 — the half-dot probe reads the wrong
slope that far out); the swim holds 94-103% inside its bound.

**The FIELD diag band** — v1 page-field parity, feature for feature:
fixed ×50 exaggeration (v1's own min(hint, cap) expression — holo3 had
dropped the hint term, which is why it saturated), two-block arrow
clamp (ruled: strict v1 fidelity; measured byte-identical to the
tighter form on every realistic field), refused planes dimmed,

## The scheduler and infrastructure

**Colour-lane repricing.** The conflation of assembly-for-new-windows
with head re-fits (measured at 31% of the seven-capture H run's CPU)
split: assembly debt fires at 15+rung applying the STANDING head;
re-fits pay 160 × the store-held count of head fits actually
performed (never the bookmark's raw ordinal; an ordinal of zero still
prices a first re-fit in full). Colour certificate latency 95.2% ->
10.6% of placement (hard sim 100% -> 18.5%), fresh fail-then-pass on
the merged tree; every re-fit fire now carries zero assembly debt.

**Done-geometry.** window_fully_done — exact, counter-derived, AND
across streams — consulted in place/calibrate/revisit eligible.
Certificate-set identity proven on all five sims both trees; work
only shrinks; 0.7 µs per pop against the 95.5 µs consult it replaces.
Tile rows are never suppressed by it.

**The lean fact-snapshot resume.** facts.npz (compressed, 9.2-9.3×)
written best-effort at RunDone; --resume folds the durable families
through the ordinary merge path in canonical order — births, wakes and
derived cells re-derive because folding IS the resume; rework is
bounded structurally (done-geometry + certified refusals + decision-
cell no-ops): 0.185× the jobs on K, 0.333× colour, byte-identical
closes; add-a-sheet and add-a-plugin proven. No guards of any kind by
ruling; AttemptFact deliberately excluded (a replayed attempt ahead of
its evidence would orphan its retry row — pinned). A replay event
keeps diag honest ("replayed N certificates · this run …"). First
real use: machine 2 sheet-I snapshot (5,543 certificates, 1.7 GB)
resuming on machine 4/machine 2 against the current tree.
