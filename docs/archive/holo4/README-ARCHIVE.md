# holo4 — the founding note, archived

**Status: ARCHIVED 2026-09-13, superseded.** The directory `holo4/` was
a place to write, not a receiver: a 2026-08-31/09-01 Grok Build
session's founding note on why three receivers existed and what a
fourth would have to be, plus one note per proposed module. No code was
ever written there; nothing here was executed. It sat untracked at the
repo root until 2026-09-13, when its retained pieces moved into docs/
and the rest came here verbatim, paths and all — `holo3/` in these
pages is `src/holo/receiver/` since 2026-09-05, and `holo2/` and
`src/holo/receive` were deleted that day.

What was retained, and where it went:

- `docs/PRINT.md`, the RFC-shaped description of the printed format for
  a second generator, is now `docs/PRINTED-FORMAT.md`, corrected against
  the format authority (its §16 lists the corrections). It is not
  duplicated here.
- The 2026-08-31 in-flight cost snapshot of the sheet-H r0850 decode
  (`CHARTER.md` §2: per-plugin cpu-s per certificate, the local row
  holding 13 of 14 workers) is carried in
  `experiments/036-sheet-h-full-readout.md`, "The 09-01 run in flight".
- The pricing proposal (`docs/engine.md` §3 and the four obligations:
  the engine owns the contract with cost) is recorded as a proposal in
  `src/holo/receiver/docs/conversion_docs/OPEN-2026-08-31.md`, Watch
  items.

Everything else — the retrospective on the three receivers, the
retirement-ratchet argument, the proposed two-part retirement bar, the
isolation map and the module stances — is superseded restatement of
tracked design and rulings, kept only as the record of what was written.
`data.md`'s field key and placement ordinal are the ones the PAGE-FIELD
line overturned (docs/PAGE-FIELD-DESIGN.md §1, the 2026-09-09 R6).
