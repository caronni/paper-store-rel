## 1. The problem

The M281 re-screens a composite colour page: a DeviceCMYK 1-bit page comes out
with the engine's device screens (165 lpi ±14° in C/M, 212 lpi 45° in Y) laid
over our cells; C survives (high contrast), M and Y do not (e038: sheet F, c3
cmy8, 941/2,520 tiles; M and Y certify only in one 4–5 mm band at ~108–113 mm).
The receiver-side ceiling on that print is below the rate (e038's supervised
classifiers), so the fix had to be upstream of the scanner: a job path that
reaches the drum unscreened, or a content the engine does not screen.

## 2. Engine facts (measured; do not re-derive)

- **600×8 contone engine, screen in the video path.** Device id `RES=600x8`.
  The PostScript interpreter (version 3017, LL3) renders into an RGB contone
  page device (`ProcessColorModel DeviceRGB`, `PixelDepth 8`); the engine
  converts and screens below every PDL. Values 0 and 255 pass the screen
  unchanged; anything the colour path moves off 0/255 is screened.
- **Nothing job-level in PostScript moves the screen.** PJL `BITSPERPIXEL=1`
  (S1), `sethalftone`/`setscreen` (honoured by the interpreter, screened below
  it — S6 witnesses), `setpagedevice` DeviceCMYK/PixelDepth 1 (accepted, no
  effect), every HP operator (adaptive halftoning, trapping, ColorSmart
  matching and adjustment, the halftone selection Smooth/Detail, the neutral
  axis), media profiles (S7a Heavy, S7b "Cardstock" — not a PPD name, a no-op).
  Turning ColorSmart off (S6) made the page lighter and the screen stronger.
  The PPD's bare names (`Smooth`, `NoAdj`, `TonerBlack` …) resolve to strings
  on the device; any string is accepted unvalidated.
- **PCL 5c device CMY (CID `Esc*v6W 01 02 01 01 01 01`, S3c)** enters the same
  pipeline: screened like PostScript, more yellow, no K substitution for CMY.
- **PCL XL**: the halftone-method operator SELECTS among the engine's screen
  sets (eHighLPI → a 188 lpi set at ±71°/±19°; a downloaded 1×1 matrix is
  ignored and the default set returns). eNoTreatment does nothing measurable
  on single-colour marks. Only eGray/eRGB colour spaces; a per-object job of
  1,168 small images with PushGS/PopGS, vector paths, a colour-space value 6
  and direct 1-bit pixels CRASHED the formatter (HP 49 error) — design jobs
  for this firmware by a novelty budget against a stream that printed.
- **PWG raster cmyk_8 over 9100**: the personality exists and refuses a CMYK
  page header (`PWGRasterPageHeader line 551 InternalError 0xc8` on the back
  channel). No device-CMYK door exists on this firmware.
- **Single toners can be laid SOLID; overlaps cannot.** In device RGB the
  engine lays cyan solid at its corner (0,255,255) on PCL XL, but on
  PostScript the corner is screened (lattice 97–130) whether matching is on or
  off — the solid PostScript cyans are (0,208,255) and (0,192,255). Yellow is
  solid at (255,255,0). Magenta is NOT solid at its corner (255,0,255) (a
  screened partial M, lattice 60–120) but solid along an edge R ∈ {240,255},
  B ∈ [128,192]: (240,0,192) is the mildest, (240,0,208) the least-yellow value
  that is just solid. K through the toner-black neutral axis is solid. Every
  two-toner overlap (CM, CY, MY) is screened partial toner at every swept value
  (red at its corner borderline); CMY is a three-toner screened composite, not
  K. Measured on the solid bars of S10p2/S11/S12/S14 by the engine's lattice
  at its known frequency and angle (floor ≤ 18).
- **The screener is a per-region switch, not a page property.** On full-width
  noise bands (S14) a cmy4 band is never screened; a cmy8 band is screened
  everywhere EXCEPT x 12–20 mm and 108–117 mm; cmyk5 bands except 12–35 mm and
  108–142 mm; the cell error follows the screen column for column. HYPOTHESIS:
  column regions with edges at the page left and at ~108 mm (the stripe seen
  on every sheet since e038) start unscreened and a content-driven switch
  turns screening on after a content-dependent distance (~8 mm cmy8, ~25–35 mm
  cmyk5, never for cmy4 over 186 mm). HP's US9171239B2 describes a columnar
  pipeline with per-region state. What flips the switch is open (colour
  count? overlap pixels? K pixels?) — S16's question. It is NOT
  information density (cmy4 at c2 carries more bits/mm² than cmyk5 at c3 and
  is never screened), NOT the object split (24 abutting tiles = one image,
  for cmy8 and cmyk5, S14/S15), NOT colour matching, NOT the media profile.
  S15: white column gaps (1 and 8 cells) do NOT reset the switch once on; the
  regions are PAGE-FIXED (an image starting at 50 mm still meets the 107 mm
  boundary); white BEFORE the content in a region lengthens the unscreened
  run (~10 mm after a 12 mm margin, ~43 mm after 50 mm: ~0.9 mm per mm of
  white, two points). S16 (041.5m): the COLOUR COUNT is refuted (a 5-entry
  lookup with an unused entry, 0.5 % red cells and 0.5 % K cells never
  screen); the switch counts COMPOSITE PIXELS (toner overlaps or K) per
  page-fixed region — lead-in × content fraction = 270–660 %·mm in every
  band and both regions (cmy8 50 %, red 20 %, K 10 % and 20 %); red spends
  what K spends (red ran under ProcBlack, K under TonerBlack — unseparated
  from the neutral call, 041.5m); K from a separate imagemask counts the same (the mechanism is NOT
  separated: the mask's K spending the budget, or stacked objects triggering
  the re-screen — 041.5m reading 2, corrected 2026-10-11); TonerBlack alone does nothing; a 20 mm
  object-free white block AFTER the switch turns it off again for ~20 mm
  (a run consistent with a reset — the ~1 mm per mm rate is S15's, before the
  switch; 041.5m reading 3, qualified 2026-10-11). Every pixel a single toner ⇒ never screened, at
  any density (dot-mixed cmy8, T2). A reading of three sheets, not a
  mechanism.
- **Plane registration.** Per-plane poses on S11/S14: M − C within ±1 px
  (2400 dpi; 4 px = 1 dot), Y − C −3…+3 px varying across the page, K − C −2
  to −5 px vertical. At c2 (8 px cells) this exceeds half a cell: c2 colour
  needs one pose per plane (the receiver's page-field line already fits one
  field per plane). "Yellow starvation" was a wrong reading of this: yellow
  alone on white reads flat across the page.
- **Route**: PostScript ≥ PCL XL for the solid alphabets (S12 vs S11: cmy4 c3
  0.36 % vs 1.4 % patch state error, single pose). Production stays
  PostScript: an 8-bit indexed DeviceRGB image with the solid palette and the
  HP operators in the prologue; the cyan entry (0,208,255).

## 4. Instruments (all in `measure/`, standalone, `PYTHONPATH=src:.`)

- `e041_screen_map.py` — per 8 mm block, the two KNOWN lattices' peak over the
  ring median (lattice-specific: valid at any cell pitch). The band/stripe maps.
- `e041_gt_compare.py` — receiver-free comparison of a full-page scan with its
  GT: orientation, six-anchor affine, per-tile FFT registration, per-state RGB
  table, per-plane d'/hard error, lateral profile. Single pose (M/Y refined
  ±6 px).
- `e041_spectrum.py` — pixel crops + 2-D spectra with FREE peak search (finds
  lattices the map does not know, e.g. the 188 lpi set).
- `e041_screen_fine.py` — generic 140–240 lpi annulus statistic in 0.7 mm
  blocks. NOT VALID AT THE c3 PITCH (an unscreened c3 print reads 34, like
  screened ones; the c2 control read 20): use only for c2 prints, or the
  lattice-specific tools. Kept as the record of the mistake.
- `e041_band_screen.py` — per 2.7 mm column of a full-width band, the three
  known lattices in R/G/B; the screen-vs-x profile that found the regions.
- Solid-bar lattice method (inside `e041_probe_readout.py`/`e041_scale_readout.py`;
  first as an inline script, `scratch/e041/probe/S10p2-bar-screen.json`):
  on a uniform bar the known lattice's peak over its ring median is a clean
  solid-vs-screened test (floor ≤ 18; scrambled 6–9; paper 19).
- `e041_probe_readout.py` (+ `e041_probe_synth.py`) — the RGB probe: fiducial
  fit (either orientation), per-patch registration, per-cell sampling,

- `e041_mixture_readout.py` (+ `e041_mixture_synth.py`) — alphabet patches:

- `e041_scale_readout.py` (+ `e041_scale_synth.py`) — full-width bands tiled
  into windows, the same classifier with per-plane poses, per-band and per-x/y

- `scripts/print_plane.sh` on the branch: `PRINT_NC_WAIT` and
  `PRINT_BACKCHANNEL` hold the socket and capture the printer's back channel

## 5. Capacity — what the alphabets deliver (discernible bits/mm²)

600 dpi = 558 dots/mm²; cells/mm²: c1 558, c2 139.5, c3 62, c4 34.9. Bits per
cell: K 1, cmy4 2, cmyk5 2.32, cmy8 3. "Discernible" = the mutual information
of the measured state confusion (hard decisions, no context) per cell × cells
per mm²; a LOWER bound; no rung, pilots, dust or furniture. The holo rungs use
55–85 % of a cell's raw bits, so net payload is roughly 0.5–0.75 of these.

with a context-free single-cell classifier — twice K c2, four times K c3, and

## 6. Avenues and how they pay

**A. The cmy4 format (the open road).** c3 lossless at page scale; nothing
more to measure before a real page. Two bits per cell as a 4-ary symbol,
carried as TWO LABEL-BIT STREAMS per tile (not toner planes: a cmy4 cell has
exactly one toner, so per-toner planes would be constrained; the label bits are
free) — cmy8's codec with n_streams = 2: per-stream codewords, the pooled
fountain, per-stream certificates, the column-group pool if the rungs differ,
and a 4-state demapper in place of the 8-state one. Labelling from S14's c2
band confusion (rows true W/C/M/Y; W's errors go to Y 4.3 %, M 2.4 %, C 1.3 %,
ink → white rare but Y → W 1.0 %): W=00, C=11, M=10, Y=01 (cyan = white's
antipode). Per-bit hard error 1.43 % / 2.03 %; I(bit) 0.897 / 0.863; decoded
independently (BICM) 1.760 b/cell, decoded in two stages (certify one stream,
demap the other with its bits known: W-vs-Y or C-vs-M) 1.797 = the full
symbol MI; the labelling moves BICM only between 1.72 and 1.76. At the
frozen ladder's 0.07–0.11 bit gap: r0700 on both streams safe, r0775 on the
first, r0775–r0850 on the conditioned second — before any context detector or
4800 capture. Word sites as white vs solid
cyan (the strongest single-toner contrast) until K is cleared; the solid
palette C (0,208,255), M (240,0,192) or (240,0,208), Y (255,255,0); per-plane
page fields (existing). [2026-10-11: superseded for the `cmy` family by
docs/CMY-DESIGN.md v0.2 §2.5. Word '1' = C; word '0' = the site's
keystream-preselected state from {W, Y} (domain 9); dust follows the balance
law. R14 (owner, 2026-10-10, "no K for now") puts no K word or dust site in the
cmy -v1 rows and keeps K as a furniture-law option for a later generation, not
a ban, so "until K is cleared" no longer describes a plan.] Estimated net
(A4-4mm, 10 % repair tiles, two-thirds of
the cmy8 row at the same geometry): c3 at r0850 631 kB/page = 91 b/mm² (capped
by the ladder's top rung — the page is lossless; a ~0.95 rung would give ~100);
c2 1.20 MB (r0700, 167 b/mm²) to 1.41 MB (r0775 + r0850, 196 b/mm²).
(Basis note: every b/mm² here is per CELL FIELD — c2 57,579 mm², c3 55,494 mm²
on A4-4mm; the e033/e036 AltaLink figures, e.g. sheet H's 284.9, are per PAGE
area, 62,370 mm² — 308.6 per field. Compare like with like.) Dot-mixed cmy8 at
c3 (avenue B): 947 kB (r0850, 136) at ~1 % per plane —
between c2-cmyw and c2-K, on the robust c3 footing. Black on
the same footing: c2-K 600 kB (r0700, 83) / 740 kB (r0850, 103, not yet shown
on this printer); c3-K 313 kB (45). M281 record today: 64.7 (e029, lower
source fraction). c2 needs the context detector on
its one-sided errors, per-plane poses (half-cell drift), and benefits from
4800 dpi (e034's "it adds"). Format work: a new family, layout_version bump,
generator palette-image emission (indexed DeviceRGB; the holo generator
emits PS already), receiver state model. Scan S14's c2 band at 4800 first
(no paper).
(2026-10-11 note: S17 has answered that step (041.5p, §7). For the
context-free classifier, scan mode changes no cell state: every band agrees
within 0.5 pp, c2 included. 4800 stays a RECEIVER lever. It gives sub-dot pose
for the pose-limited layout reading (U4–U6 4–8 pp), and e034's added 4800
capture bought 14 of the last 17 tile-streams (CMY-DESIGN §9, §14.1). holo has
no layout version (HOLO-DESIGN.md:37-38). In holo terms, this avenue's
"layout_version bump" means a new Family token plus gen v1 plus new goldens
(CMY-DESIGN §5). For §6B's bump the question is still open: CMY-DESIGN §9.1
leaves the dot-mixed overlap layout's choice between a route and a
printed-format change to be settled when that route is picked up.)

Archival standing of c3-cmy4 (open). e041 names c2-K "the archival row"
(§041.5o item 5). c3-cmy4 reads 0.06–0.33 % on four sheets (S14–S17;
CMY-DESIGN §1.3) at about twice c3-K's density (123.5 vs 62 b/mm²
discernible; estimated 631 vs 313 kB/page net), single toners only. Calling
it archival-grade colour holds on two conditions. (a) The colour toners have
to last, and that was never measured here: DESIGN.md §2.2 only tabulates
typical pigment classes (C PB15:3 and M PR122 class at Blue Wool 7–8,
"Near-K"; Y azo/benzimidazolone ≈ 5–7, "Weakest link"). (b) The per-plane
poses have to hold, because even c3 reads 3.3 % on a single pose (S15 R8,
0.33 % per plane). Single prints, NO promotion.

**B. DOT-MIXED colour at c3 — the second road, measured (S16, S17).** S17: the
7-STATE alphabet (no three-toner cell) reads 0.54 % at page scale with the
same discernible bits as the 8-state (170 b/mm²) — that is the row: the
reduced-alphabet erasure row at r0775 with erasures ≈ 860 kB/page (124
net), robust. ORIENTATION of the overlap cell's layout is a usable symbol
where the per-plane pose is good (1.7–2.8 %): 7 states + one orientation bit
on the two-toner cells = 3.00 b/cell = 186 b/mm² discernible, ~1.0 MB/page
(144 net) at 2.4 % / 2.5 % — the layout decision is pose-limited (U4/U6
10–19 % with x-structure and a correlation with |M−C|, |Y−C|), so a local
pose refinement is the readout lever. PHASE (a one-dot shift) is not
readable at this registration (12–18 %). c2 dot-mixed is CLOSED (18–24 % for
every pair layout; the 2+2 cells read 73 %). The S16 paragraph follows. The screener
spends a per-region budget on composite pixels; a cmy8 page whose overlap
cells are drawn from single-toner dots (DOTMIX3: CM = 5 C + 4 M in the 3×3,
CMY by (r+c) mod 3) has none and is unscreened everywhere: 3.22 % state
error at page scale, 2.77 of 3 b/cell = 171.8 b/mm² discernible; per plane
~1 % (the three-toner cell is the weak state, 85 %). That is the EXISTING
c3-cmy8 format at r0850 — 947 kB per A4 page, 136 b/mm² net — with a
generator-side change (the overlap cells' dot layout; a printed-format
change, layout_version bump) and the receiver's cell model told that an
overlap plane covers half the cell. The reduced-alphabet erasure row
(7 states, no CMY) has its first measured customer here. Owed: a real page
through holo3 (cell model), the dot layouts' effect on the per-plane
registration reading. c2 is NOT a candidate (S12: 22–30 %).
Scope: dot-mixing is the M281's answer to its composite-pixel screener.
Engines that lay overlap cells unscreened already close plain c2-cmy8 above
it: the AltaLink at r0850 (e036, 308.6 b/mm² per field = 284.9 per page area)
and the Canon at r0700 / r0775 (e040: 1,598,326 / 1,785,886 B). On the
AltaLink, dot-mixed pairs do not read at c2. Their orientation reads 0.98 % at
c3, which confirms the M281's symbol, but c3 stays below c2 on that engine.
There the lever is the receiver, not the alphabet (e042 §042.4).

**B'. Steer the screener — closed as a capacity route.** Gaps and object
edges do NOT reset the switch (S15); white before or after the content buys
~1 mm of run per mm (S15; S16 T13 confirms refill after the switch, yes/no
only): at best half content for cmy8 (~80 b/mm²)
— below cmy4 c3's 123.5. A K overlay from a separate object counts like
image K (S16 T9). Sparse K or red cells (≤ 0.5 %) on a cmyw page cost
nothing and read solid: K furniture is available. (The colour-count hypothesis that once stood
here — 4 colours never screen, 5 and 8 do — was REFUTED by S16, §2: the
trigger is composite pixels; S16's band list is in the log, 041.5l.)
K as a DATA state in place of the three-toner cell is closed here by the same
budget: at 1/8 of the cells it makes ~12.5 % of the page composite pixels,
between S16's T7 (cmyw + 10 % K, on at ~39 mm, 27 mm lead-in) and T8 (cmyk5,
20 % K in one image, on at ~36 mm), and 270–660 %·mm puts the switch ~22–53 mm
into each region (an estimate, not printed): cmyk5's page-scale collapse
(§5). The three-toner cell stays dot-mixed. R14's no-K-furniture ruling
(CMY-DESIGN §12) is a separate question and explicitly not a screening one;
on the AltaLink K as the eighth state measured bit-neutral at c2 (e042 X3,
K ↔ CM).

**C. Composite cells (unexplored beyond the dot-mixed overlaps).** The engine
prints our dots bit-faithfully, so the cell's interior is ours: (i) amplitude
by dot count — 0/3/6/9 dots of one toner per c3 cell, a ten-state alphabet
(3.3 bits/cell) if the levels separate after dot gain (C has d' ~10 to spend,
M ~6, Y ~3); (ii) guard rings — a 2×2 core in a 3-dot pitch, or four cores in
a 5-dot super-cell, trading density for the separability c2 lacks. The
readouts need nothing new; the format needs the state-to-bit mapping.
(iii) A majority bit in the two-toner dot-mixed cells: 5 C + 4 M vs 4 C + 5 M,
one more bit per cell. Untested; the expectation (an estimate) is that it does
not separate. The swap is a one-dot coverage step, finer than T10's three-dot
amplitude steps, and adjacent T10 levels already confuse 8–15 % (S16). On the
AltaLink, e042 X8 reads the mixed state as its majority single at 6.8–8.3 %
(CMx → C 8.3, CYx → C 6.8, MYx → M 7.1) because the engine under-prints the
4-dot minority toner.

**D. Closed, by measurement.** PostScript graphics-state and HP-operator
doors; media profiles; PCL 5c device CMY; PWG CMYK; the RGB value as the
lever for overlaps; colour matching; object splitting; the transfer-nip
reading of the stripe (superseded by the region reading); firmware/driver
reverse engineering judged not to pay (a bypass would yield less than cmy4-c2
unless overlaps print solid, and no device-CMYK path exists). Worth one
afternoon each, read-only: a driver-output capture under every HP driver
setting; a strings pass over the firmware update file for hidden PJL/PS
settings (DefaultScreening, phases).

**D'. cmy at c1: closed by argument, not printed.** The owner asked on
2026-10-05 about c1-K or c1-cmyw at a very low rate that still beats c2-cmyw
in b/mm². A cmy c1 cell is one dot of one toner, so it is an isolated dot
wherever its neighbours hold another state. A lone 1-dot does not print on
this engine (e001.1: lone 1-dot K ink 0.000; e031 §8: 1.3 % contrast). That
was measured on K only, before the toner swap; single colour dots have not
been measured. The per-plane registration (|Y − C| up to 2.35 dots, S17 U12,
§7) also moves a plane by more than two c1 cells. So the error depends on the
content and is not random: an isolated ink dot reads white, and "558 cells × 2
bits at a low rate" does not apply. Only a code that never writes an isolated
dot gets around this. Its K version (e003's 2-D RLL i2w3, B2: 82–86 b/mm²)
sits below c2-K's 93–109, and the colour version is an unbuilt design study.
c1-K is closed by the same fact (041.5g).

**E. cmy7 — DEAD (operator's ruling 2026-10-05).** The triple-free and
packed-7 lines are closed; the independent-plane generation findings keep
their value for cmy4/cmyk5.

## 7. Open questions and caveats (the honest list)

- Everything is one sheet per condition, two days, one toner state. The
  first owed control is S14 printed again on another day.
- The screener trigger is measured as a composite-pixel budget per
  page-fixed region (S16); what sets the region boundaries (page left,
  ~107 mm) and the budget's exact form (running sum vs running average,
  hysteresis) is not. White credit: ~1 mm per mm before the switch (S15); after it,
  refill yes, rate unmeasured (S16 T13, yes/no by design), object-free in both cases; in-image white gaps ≤ 3.4 mm
  do not count (S15 R4). The 108 mm boundary's origin is unknown
  (raster-fixed per S8; a column-region edge by the S14 profile). The three
  black bars' toner identity (mask neutral; the two image blacks identical
  and slightly blue) wants a loupe.
- Amplitude cells (T10) and the whole-cell feature: adjacent levels confuse
  8–15 %; the whole-cell box reads worse than the core — composite cells are
  parked below dot-mixed cmy8.
- THE 4800 QUESTION, answered (S17 at 2400 and 4800, 041.5p): the scan mode
  changes no cell state (every band within 0.5 pp, c2 included — the
  scanner's ~860 dpi ceiling); 4800 helps only the pose-limited layout
  reading (U4–U6 4–8 pp better). cmyw c2 tracks the PRINT's per-plane
  registration: 2.82 / 3.86 / 4.00 / 6.5 % at |Y − C| 0.49 / 0.67 / 1.13 /
  2.35 dots on four prints (S17's 6.5 % is the print; its band sits where
  S16's did — an earlier "page bottom" reading was a band-index slip,
  withdrawn). c2 colour is registration-limited print to print; levers =
  per-plane page field + context detector; scan resolution changes no cell
  state for the context-free classifier, but 4800 stays a RECEIVER lever —
  the sub-dot pose above, and on the AltaLink's sheet I one added 4800
  capture bought 14 of the last 17 tile-streams after three 2400s were
  exhausted (e034; aligned with CMY-DESIGN §9 and §14.1, 2026-10-11).
- A DOT-LEVEL CONSTRAINED CODE was named in the 2026-10-05 session as the
  ceiling: a research line, not a sheet (an analysis recommendation; there is
  no owner ruling). The symbol is the whole 9-dot pattern of a c3 cell under a
  printability constraint (ink runs ≥ 2 dots, never an isolated dot). It
  would carry several bits per cell (an estimate, unmeasured) and is the one
  route toward c1-class density. Prerequisites: a 2-D dot-lattice receiver
  and per-plane fields at ~0.1 dot. Its one measured piece is S17's
  orientation symbol (1.7–2.8 % where poses are good). Against the premise:
  4800 changes no cell state (above; cells are soft blobs at 8 px/dot,
  041.5o), and its K ancestors failed. DESIGN v0.4's M-B run-length field
  (ink-run ≥ 2, white-run ≥ 3; 0.154 b/dot naive) got its BCJR trellis in
  e004, which gained nothing on paper (13.7 → 13.6 %) because 18.5 % of
  2-dot ink runs drop out whole, an error the constraint allows. e010's B×B
  motif dictionary has minimum distance 1, and e010 reads soft LLRs into
  the LDPC as gaining nothing from motif structure.
- The c2 colour numbers are context-free and single-cell; the production
  receiver will read better, by how much is unmeasured.
- Yellow is the weakest plane everywhere (c3 cmy4: Y 2.5 % best-threshold
  where C/M are 0); magenta's blue absorption confounds any per-plane Y
  threshold — read states, not planes, or unmix.
- Every two-toner dot-mixed cell printed so far (S16 T2, S17 U1–U11, e042
  X5/X6/X8/X13/X14) gives the first toner of the state's name the majority:
  A = C in CM and CY, M in MY, on 5 of 9 dots at c3 (X and oriented bands),
  B the other 4; 2:2 at c2. Y is never the majority toner, and C is always
  CM's. The minority under-read (U2: CM → C 0.9, CY → C 0.6, MY → M 0.5 %;
  AltaLink X8: CMx → C 8.3, CYx → C 6.8, MYx → M 7.1 %, "the blend leans to
  A") therefore cannot be told apart from a C > M > Y toner order. It is not
  Y's weakness alone: CM carries no Y and shows the largest under-read on
  both engines (one print each). Untested on either engine: a pair whose
  later toner holds the majority (Y in CY/MY, M in CM). A dot-mixed
  generator's layout table has to choose who gets the majority.
- Registration drifts per plane across the page (Y ±3 px, K −2…−5 px at
  2400 dpi); a page field per plane is required at c2.
- The matching witness: ColorSmart matching has no per-image effect on
  PostScript images here; whether it latches page-globally is untested.
- The 49-crash's exact cause inside S10p was not bisected (by decision).
- Readout tidy-ups owed (checker, 2026-10-05): the mixture readout's pose
  wording in its summary JSON; the scale readout's cost estimate; state

## 8. Lessons written into memory

- Novelty budget: a job for a real engine is designed by its distance from a
  stream that printed, and the refuter audits that list first.
- No mechanism words without a discriminating control ("yellow starvation"
  was Y-to-C misregistration through a single-pose readout).
- A generic periodic-energy statistic must be calibrated at the print's own
  cell pitch; lattice-specific statistics need no calibration.
- Composite scores (uniform-error floors) misprice alphabets whose errors
  concentrate in one plane; score with the confusion's mutual information or
  per plane.
