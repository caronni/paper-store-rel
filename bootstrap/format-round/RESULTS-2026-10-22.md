**Why the sets' pages are transformed, not regenerated.** The w4 sets render
from `build/w4/w1f4/pages/{one,two_p0,two_p1}` and `build/w4/p2f4/page_p2`.
`page_p2` was made by gen.py at 1d309208, before the copies went across pages,
so the current generator, and gen_variant with it, cannot regenerate it from
its payload. A variant differs from the record ONLY inside the 15 bullseye
blocks. So `make_pages.py` takes each frozen record page and re-draws its
bullseye blocks with gen_variant's own `RING`, `HEADER_MODULES` and `render`:
the record's 68 header bits are read off the record's header modules and put
on the variant's. The checks:

- `make_pages.py check`: transform(the generator builder's stage-1
  record-coarse page) == gen_variant's page for all 8 configurations × 3
  pages. The .grid.txt, the .pbm and the sidecar's header all match:
  **24/24 identical**.
- On every source page, the record render of the source grid == the frozen
  .pbm, and the 15 blocks' header bits agree with each other and with the
  sidecar.
- On every variant page, `turned_reads_passing` (the orientation refusal)
  is empty.
- `verify_variant.py` on the w1 pages with their payloads: timing,
  `ring bad 0`, header and pbm all checked. Its only failures are the three
  pre-amendment sidecar keys (page_step, band_groups, fourth_group). They
  are identical on record-coarse, which is the frozen page byte for byte.
- `render.py check`: record-coarse's re-renders == the frozen renders, byte
  for byte, **120 of 120**. That covers build/w4/{w1f4,sets4,p2f4}, §11's
  build/changeset/edge/renders and §14's build/changeset/onebull/renders.

**The false-seed instrument** (`seeds_fr.py`) runs each capture ALONE, in
process, the way `run_sets.slots` runs a text:

1. A spy on the finder `dd` records its candidate list. Nothing else is
   wrapped.
2. Each candidate is mapped through the capture's
   `homography_grid_modules_to_px` to grid modules. (For a loupe grid PGM
   the map is one pixel per module with six modules of border.) A candidate
   within one module (Euclidean) of a bullseye block's module (0,0) centre
   is TRUE. Every other candidate is FALSE.
3. The header is read as the capture reads it alone.
4. The walk runs from every seed. When the capture does not read the
   header, the page's own header is given (the slot pass's 'known'). A spy
   on the block read records each read's seed index (s[5][0]). A seed
   STARTED A WALK when a read carries its index.
5. The counterfactual: the same capture with the finder's list filtered to
   its true seeds, fm and the walk re-run in a fresh namespace. The
   **cost** = chunks held with the true seeds only − chunks held as found.
   It is negative when the false seeds gained chunks.

True bullseyes in frame are the centres inside the image. This excludes the
blocks the render's `be:` damage destroys, and on the one-bullseye renders
the 14 destroyed blocks. Cross-check: on all 224 one-bullseye runs (28
renders × 8 configurations) the instrument's header-read-alone equals the
process's (0 mismatches), and its chunks equal the process's wherever the
header reads.
