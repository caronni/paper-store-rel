## What this run proves, and what it does not

Proved (MEASURED, once): the printed v3 chain is self-sufficient from a.py
to the unpacked archive, byte-exact at every hand-over (a.py, b.py, c.py
sha-equal to the chain; payload.bin the T1 container); ruling 37's "at c3,
I'd posit 600 dpi is enough" holds on paper with a consumer flatbed; the
repaired c.py (8affb97b, da0cd032) reads real prints whose predecessor
found no lattice (10-23); the fallback and the idempotent re-feed work on
paper; b.py reads the R2 pages from 300 dpi grey scans.

Not proved: a second typist, a second printer, a second scanner, a photo
route for the archive pages; any other dpi on paper for this volume (1200,
2400); pages fed out of order, partial or damaged; ageing. The margin is
thin as run: 6,264 of 6,260 is where c.py STOPPED decoding (page 8), not a
spare: seven whole pages gave 5,723, so the volume needs the eighth page.
At the measured 817.6 certified a page the volume closes on 8 pages, at the
worst page's 774 on 9 (6,260 / 774 = 8.09); the 1,200 dpi simulation closes
on 8 at 840 a page (stage3/RESULTS-2026-10-22.md §3). The release as
unpacked does not run (no tables, no requirements, flat names): the archive
restores bytes, not a working holo. And the release is the record's tree of
2115a595: at HEAD 4a4b2f4d, 67 files of T1's selection differ (37
modified, 30 added; `git ls-tree` of both).
