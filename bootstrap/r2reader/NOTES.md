## How the v1 readers read (R2-FORMAT.md, Decoding)
Binarise to find bullseyes (thirteen alternating runs along a row, confirmed
along the column and by the ring pattern); the frame's rotation and quarter
turn from the joint score of every bullseye's timing frame; the header (68
bits, 20-bit check) around any seed; a breadth-first walk from every seed,
each block refined on its 68 timing modules with steps re-measured as it
goes; thresholds from the timing modules; CRC-32 accepts a block; chunks
pooled by global index across frames and pages; the data decoded to b.py
when complete. Measured limits of the typed reader: a whole page needs 2 samples per
module at σ 0.45 blur, or 1.5 at σ 0.4; tilt within 10°; perspective within
2 %; the loupe grid at 0.5 % flips; all 15 bullseyes destroyed.

# r2reader v0 notes (2026-10-18; superseded by the v1 section above, kept as the 10-18/10-19 record)

## Built
- `reader.py`: typed subset, **169 counted lines** (`count_reader.py`:
  7,447 chars, longest 72, no import/float/complex/`/`/`;`/`~`/`@`, only
  whitelisted builtins; `open`/`input` as the spec needs). Integer
  only, positions in 1/16 px. Uses bytes/list/dict methods, `lambda`.
- `test_render.py` (numpy): renderer from R2-FORMAT.md alone, camera
  simulator (homography, blur, light ±20 %, noise 10, rotation, stamps).

## How it reads
Transition-projection bbox → extreme ink pixel per corner → bullseye
search ±4 modules (comb, 4 directions, 14 scales; then 2-D ring
template) → accepted if > ½ best, else the extreme-point estimate →
BW, BH by timing agreement (25 candidates) → homography → block walk
from the best corner (plane-extrapolated offset, coarse-to-fine timing
search) → second walk with corners corrected. Decode: Laplacian
equaliser at 3 strengths, Chase over 6 least reliable modules (+ single
flips), bitwise CRC; chunks pooled across captures.

## Spec questions
1. 12 dots: "largest that fits" in 277 mm gives BH = 32 (H = 545), not
   30; S/T/capacity follow 30. Renderer uses 30; reader is generic.
2. Bullseye ring "d = 8 … row/col 0 and 16": local indices are 0..15;
   16 is the timing line, so right/bottom quiet zone is 1 module.
3. No orientation mark; centre is 9/8 modules from the edges, so a 180°
   photo is off by one module. Reader assumes upright (±4° tested).
4. 1-module rings and timing vanish at σ0.6 (spec's limit); a 2-module
   bullseye would survive.
5. PGM ≥ 2 spm (spec) vs ≥ 1.5 (task): works to 1.3.
6. Leftover slots (S − 3T = 2) presumably 0xFFFF; reader ignores index.
7. Threshold from ring quartiles, not two class medians (equal when
   balanced; one line shorter).
8. Extension: '?' in loupe grid; repeated input pools chunks.
