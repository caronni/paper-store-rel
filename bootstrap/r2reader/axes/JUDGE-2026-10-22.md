## Ruling: a code bug

The up-left-only pattern comes from the code. The format and the sampling
do not cause it.

**The source.** `ft` (reader_lite_v1.py line 89) breaks exact score ties
toward its largest candidate:

```
k,m,n=max((score,r,q) for r in(m-y,m,m+y)for q in(n-y,n,n+y))
```

On a hard-edged capture the frame score is flat within about ±16 units
(1/16 px) of the module centre. So every refined centre slides to the +x/+y
edge of that flat region, about +15 units, which is 0.31 module at 3 px per
module. The bias is fixed in image coordinates, not page coordinates.

**The selector.** `fm` (lines 144-145) breaks its rotation tie the same way.
On the grid the score is exactly 130050 at five steps, r = -128..+128, and
it picks r = +128, a turn of 1.79°. The rotated seed axes decide which image
axis the ft drift lands in, and so which diagonal crosses the half-module
line.

**The channel (where the record put the cause).** The walk learns a block's
axes as its refined centre minus its neighbour's. That turns the difference
between two drifted landing points into an axis error. It carries the error
but does not create it.

**Discriminating numbers.** All are from the stage-1 page 0 grid, the
record's "perfect grid". I re-measured each with my own runner, which
execs the reader's own code with only `ft` or `fm` swapped:

| variant | chunks held |
|---|---|
| reader as is | 595 / 609 |
| ft ties to the centre of the tied set | 609 |
| ft ties to the candidate nearest the stage centre ("stay") | 609 |
| ft ties to the smallest candidate | 583 |
| rotation forced to 0, ft as is | 593 |
| rotation forced to +64, ft as is | 609 |

So moving the tie rule alone moves or removes the loss.

The "stay" rule, as a complete reader file (/tmp/axes-judge/reader_stay.py),
gives:
- grids p0 / p1 / p2: 609 / 609 / 609 and "done", against 595 / 593 / 591
  as is;
- nominal-b030 p0: 601 against 601, unchanged.

**The quarter-turn test** (log s1-p0-grid-rot1, recounted by me). Turned
once, the page's up-left block reads 13 of 15. The page's up-right and
down-left fail 15 of 15 each. Turned three times, the page's up-right fails
15 of 15 and the page's up-left reads 15 of 15. Across the four
orientations, the image up-left fails 15, 15, 11 and 15 times. A page-fixed
cause cannot do that. That includes H5, the format's lever arm, which gives
the up-left corner the longest lever, (8, 8) modules against down-right's
(7, 7). That lever is real, but it only tilts marginal cases (8/7). It does
not select the failing class, so the verdict is "bug", not "both".

**Where the bug acts.** Only on exactly flat score surfaces:
- the 0/1 text grid;
- the exact 600-dpi raster (fm has a 7-way tie there, r = +192; as is only
  60 blocks are accepted and 427 are never visited);
- the camera.py perfect render at spm 7, where every ft call ties but the
  error stays below the read limit.

It does not act on blurred captures or photos:
- fm has no tie on any nominal render or photo.
- ft ties in only 2-6 % of calls there.
- The four diagonals are not missed at all on the nominal renders, the
  nominal spm 7 render or photos 1, 2 and 4. Photo 3 misses one up-right
  diagonal block (1, 3), 2 cells. No diagonal is preferred.
- The tie rules change nothing there: the nominal p0 / p2 chunks are
  601 / 605 under the reader, the centre rule and the "stay" rule alike,
  and photo 1 holds 204 chunks under all three.

The record's sentence ("because the walk learns a block's axes as the
difference of two fitted positions") names the channel, not the cause. Its
"about a third of a module at the far corner" is the axis share only. The
measured far-corner error is 0.54 module: +0.31 centre (tie drift),
+0.13 from the short B, +0.11 from the tilted A.

## What the residual misses on blurred captures and photos are

They are not the up-left pattern, and the tie fix does not touch them.
They are pose errors in which the LEARNED axis is several percent off.
Per unread block, best visit (all single-visit):

- **nominal-b030 p0**: 8 unread; axis errors 3.5-5.3 % on one axis in 6 of
  the 8; far corner 0.45-0.60 module in 5. For comparison, read blocks
  have far corner p50 0.12, p99 0.39, max 0.49.
- **nominal-b030 p1 / p2**: the same blocks recur across pages: (22, 2)
  5.33 %, (23, 2) 5.17 %, (11, 14) 2.61 %, (20, 0-2) 4.4-4.9 %. The camera
  warp and walk order are the same on every page (persp 0.02, seed 1), so
  this is pose geometry, not content.
- **Photos 1, 2 and 4**: 1 / 3 / 3 unread, 2-3 cells each. Photo 4's three
  have far corner 0.36-0.53 and axis errors 1.9-4.4 %.
- **Photo 3**: column 0 rows 10-14 lose the translation itself (centre
  error 0.6-1.0 module, 4-105 cells misread). That is an edge-of-walk
  failure, not an axis error.

## What fix #2 must do

Fix #2 is each block's pose fitted from its own frame: translation and the
two axis vectors.

1. **Fit A and B per block from the block's own 68-module frame** (the
   18×18 ring at -9 and +8, a 17-module baseline), to about 1 % or better
   per axis. At a lever of 8 modules, 1 % is 0.08 module, so axis share
   plus centre error stays well under the half module. The measured misses
   on nominal renders sit at 3.5-5.3 %; that is what fix #2 must remove.
2. **No one-sided tie-break in any of its searches**: translation, scale
   and shear or rotation. Use the centre of the tied set or the candidate
   nearest the current estimate. Otherwise, on hard captures, each search
   adds up to the half-width of the flat region (±16 units at 3 px per
   module) to the axes, and the grid failure comes back in a new place.
3. **Re-fit the seed's pose from its frame like any other block.** Today
   the seed keeps fm's 0.9°-quantized rotation, a tied rotation on hard
   captures (3.07 % axis error on the grid), and the bullseye-finder
   centre; every dist-1 block's axes inherit that mix.
4. **Keep the learned axes only as the prediction for the neighbour**, not
   as the reading pose, or combine the two only through a fit that cannot
   pass a translation drift into the axes.
5. **Re-measure on the same set**: grids p0 / p1 / p2 (must stay 609),
   exact raster, perfect spm 2 / 3 / 7, nominal p0 / p1 / p2 (8 / 5 / 4
   unread today), nominal spm 7, the four photos. Audit the typed cost
   with count_reader.py.

## A smaller fix

It already removes the measured up-left error (all of the grid misses). It
does not remove the residual blurred-capture misses.

ft's tie rule, preferring the candidate nearest the stage centre. The
reader_lite_v1.py lines 89-90 become:

```
  k,k,m,n=max((sum(v(x+r,y+q)*t for(x,y),t in c),-abs(r-m)-abs(q-n)
   ,r,q)for r in(m-y,m,m+y)for q in(n-y,n,n+y))
```

- count_reader.py: chars 4404 → 4425 (+21), longest line 71.
- Measured: grids 609 / 609 / 609 "done" (as is 595 / 593 / 591); exact
