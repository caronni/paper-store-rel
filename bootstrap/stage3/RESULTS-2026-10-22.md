### The design as built (c_readable.py)

- **The address word is the arbiter.** `Scan.find_lattice` is a generator:
  it yields candidate lattices, start point by start point, and
  `Receiver.read_scan` tries `place_page` on each until one places, and
  the search goes on past one that places nothing. "no cell lattice found"
  = nothing yielded, "no tile address found" = nothing placed. Both floors
  are 1.2. The cost of a page with no lattice on it: `place_page` on a
  candidate that places nothing is ~1 s (nine `first_tile` blocks of
  0.1 s), but each `Scan.track` that made the candidate costs 1–10 s on the
  text page; the bound is 9 start points × 3 trial pitches = 27 tracks and
  at most 27 `place_page` calls, measured 146–165 s on the listing sheet.
  The reader takes the FIRST candidate that places ≥ 3 tiles, not the best
  one (owed below).
- **Trial pitches** 1, 0.96, 1.04 × the declared one, per start point; the
  coarse lock over a 17 × 17-cell window (`phase(lat, 0, 0, 8, 1)`, the same
  289 samples as the old 24/3): it finds the angle to ±1° with the pitch
  ±4–5 % off (`lock_sweep.py`: right angle at e = −5 … +5 % on every scan
  and start point but a few at ±4/5 %). The three trials cover about ±8 %
  (ESTIMATE; measured: hints −4 % and +5 % on a 100 % print, and the 96.3 %
  print at its declared dpi), and that coverage rests on the address word,
  not on the walk: see the next item.
- **The pitch is measured by the walk.** The ring walk already measures the
  cell phase's drift from the lattice's prediction and refits at every ring;
  it now starts at 4 cells instead of 8, so a 4 % pitch error is a 0.16-cell
  drift at the first ring. The brief's explicit two-step drift (N = 8, then
  N = 32) was measured and NOT built: on `03-b-1200.pgm` the N = 8 points
  put the j step at 5.90 px against the true 5.998 (the phase scatters by
  ~0.15 cell per point; sd 0.16–0.33 cell over random cells of the fitted
  lattice on every scan — the pixma pages are not affine at the cell level),
  and the N = 32 points then sat at +0.42 / −0.48 cell, at the wrap. The
  walk refines a pitch a few percent off; it does not guarantee it: a
  ~5 % trial can converge wrong on one axis and pass the square check (the
  +5 % row below: the ×1.00 trial converged to steps 6.01 / 6.22 px, 3.7 %
  off on one axis, within the 6 % check). The address word rejects such a
  candidate and the next trial pitch reads the page, so the ~±8 % coverage
  rests on the arbiter.
- The square check (12 points, both steps within 6 %, 2° of a right angle)
  is relative to the trial pitch. `Scan.aperture(p)` sets the box from a
  pitch: at each trial, and from the pitch found before the candidate is
  yielded (on these scans round(0.7 p) never changed: 4 at 1200, 2 at 600).
- The "cell lattice found, placing tiles" line prints once per candidate
  tried; the module docstring says so, and that a print scaled by a few
  percent is read. (Until the third pass, below: one line, for the
  lattice that placed.)
